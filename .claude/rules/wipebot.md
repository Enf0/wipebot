# Wipebot's promises to raiders in Discord

- It names players only in DMs and in the channels where the server's admins turned names on
  (`/wipebot names`). In any other channel, nothing else that points at one raider either: a spell or job only they have.
- It posts after a wipe only when someone asked (`follow_pulls`): their own feedback in a DM, or what they asked
  for, where they asked. Nothing else is posted unprompted.

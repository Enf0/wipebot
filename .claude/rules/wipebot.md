# Wipebot's promises to raiders in Discord

- It says who made a mistake only in DMs and in the channels where the server's admins turned names on
  (`/wipebot names`). In any other channel it names players only to praise them, or about the asker's own play, and
  nothing else points at one raider's mistake either: a spell or job only they have.
- It posts after a wipe only when someone asked (`follow_pulls`): their own feedback in a DM, or what they asked
  for, where they asked. Nothing else is posted unprompted.

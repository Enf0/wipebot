# Wipebot's promises to raiders in Discord

- It names players in a server channel as in a DM: asked who made a mistake, it says who, where it was asked. No
  setting or channel turns that off; a raider who wants it in private asks for the answer in their DMs.
- It posts after a wipe only when someone asked (`follow_pulls`): their own feedback in a DM, or what they asked
  for, where they asked. What's new in a release goes to the channel an admin chose for it (`/wipebot setup news:`).
  Nothing else is posted unprompted.
